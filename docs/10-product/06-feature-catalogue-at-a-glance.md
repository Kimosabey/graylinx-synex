# 6. Feature Catalogue at a Glance

| **Feature area** | **What the user can do** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| Synex Copilot | Ask, explain, investigate, compare, recommend, draft, act, verify and summarize. | “Why is Chiller-03 unhealthy?” |
| AI Reports | Create, explain, compare, drill down and schedule reports using natural language. | “Give me this week's maintenance summary.” |
| AI Work Orders | Create WOs from chat/Fault Detection and Diagnosis (FDD), summarize, prioritize, plan, assign, assist and verify. | “Create a WO for this fault.” |
| Equipment AI | Ask about any allowed equipment's health, history, faults and repairs. | “Has this happened before?” |
| Reliability AI | Explain FDD/ML findings, find similar cases, trends and repeat failures. | “Why did FDD raise this?” |
| Knowledge AI | Ask manuals, SOPs, previous WOs and approved documents questions. | “What is the inspection procedure?” |
| Planning AI | Suggest what work to do first, when, and who can do it. | “What can we schedule tomorrow?” |
| Parts / Inventory AI | Check parts, reservations, shortages and delivery impact. | “Can we finish this WO tomorrow?” |
| Smart Alerts | Group, explain, prioritize and route important alerts. | “Why did I get this alert?” |
| Technician AI | Give job context, safety guidance, procedures, history and findings capture. | “What should I check first?” |
| Customer AI | Let customers ask about only their sites and equipment. | “How is my plant performing?” |
| Executive AI | Give short business briefings with drill-down. | “What needs my attention?” |
| Verification AI | Check whether work actually fixed the issue. | “Did the repair work?” |
| Safety AI | Explain safety requirements and block controlled unsafe actions. | “Can I perform this action?” |
