# 25. Product North Star

**Graylinx should feel like one intelligent maintenance assistant for the whole organization: from the executive asking a business question, to the reliability engineer investigating a fault, to the technician fixing the equipment.**

The AI should make existing Graylinx data, ML and workflows easier to understand and use — and then connect them into a controlled loop:

ASK → UNDERSTAND → FIND PROOF → EXPLAIN → RECOMMEND → APPROVE → WORK → VERIFY → REPORT → LEARN
