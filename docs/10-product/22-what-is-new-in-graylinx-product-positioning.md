# 22. What Is New in Graylinx — Product Positioning

| **Area** | **Traditional system** | **New Graylinx AI experience** |
|---|---|---|
| **Reports** | Open dashboard and filter | Ask a question and get a grounded answer |
| **Work Orders** | Manually create and manage | AI prepares, explains, prioritizes and verifies |
| **Equipment** | Open many screens | Ask the equipment directly |
| **FDD** | Technical fault output | Simple explanation + proof + next action |
| **Knowledge** | Search documents | Ask questions across approved knowledge |
| **Planning** | Planner manually compares many factors | AI suggests the best options |
| **Alerts** | Many separate notifications | AI groups, explains and prioritizes |
| **Customers** | Static reports | Interactive customer AI assistant |
| **Executives** | Many dashboards | Short AI briefing + drill-down |
| **Technicians** | Manual searching and writing | Job-aware AI guidance and structured findings |
| **Verification** | Often manual closure | AI helps prove whether the repair worked |
| **Platform control** | Rules spread across systems | Central AI Control Plane |
