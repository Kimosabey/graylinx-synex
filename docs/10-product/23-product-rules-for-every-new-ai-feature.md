# 23. Product Rules for Every New AI Feature

- Make the user task easier, not more complicated.
- Use existing Graylinx data and ML as the foundation.
- Show the reason/proof for important AI answers.
- Never invent a diagnosis when the evidence is not enough.
- Never let AI bypass access, safety or approval rules.
- Let users move from answer → detail → action without losing context.
- For actions, show what will happen before it happens when approval is required.
- Always verify important maintenance outcomes.
- Keep a record of important AI decisions and actions.
