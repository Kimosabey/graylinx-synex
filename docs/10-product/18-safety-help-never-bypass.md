# 18. Safety — Help, Never Bypass

| **Case** | **AI behavior** |
|---|---|
| **User asks for unsafe action** | Block or route to authorized human |
| **Permit required** | Show permit requirement and approval path |
| **Technician lacks required qualification** | Do not authorize controlled action |
| **Document conflicts with safety rule** | Safety rule wins; show the conflict |
| **Emergency** | Use defined emergency workflow; record who approved what |

Simple rule: AI can explain safety requirements, prepare work and help people follow procedures. AI must not secretly bypass safety controls.
