# 1. What We Are Giving Users

The first thing the document should explain is what people can actually do with the new Graylinx AI platform. The architecture comes after that. The main customer experience is built around three core areas:

| **Priority** | **Core feature** | **Simple promise** |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Reports | Ask questions, understand what is happening, investigate why, and create useful reports. |
| P0 | Work Orders | Create, plan, execute, track and verify maintenance work with AI assistance. |
| P0 | Synex Copilot | One assistant that can understand the user, find the right information, explain it and help take approved actions. |
