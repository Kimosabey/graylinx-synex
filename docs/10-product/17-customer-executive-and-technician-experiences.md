# 17. Customer, Executive and Technician Experiences

| **Experience** | **New AI feature** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | Ask about site performance | “Why did my site's availability fall?” |
| **Customer** | Ask about open work | “What work is planned for this week?” |
| **Customer** | Report explanation | “Summarize this month's issues.” |
| **Executive** | Daily business briefing | “What needs my attention?” |
| **Executive** | Risk explanation | “Why is this site high risk?” |
| **Executive** | Decision support | “Where should we spend maintenance budget?” |
| **Technician** | Job briefing | “Give me the important points before I start.” |
| **Technician** | Live guidance | “What should I check next?” |
| **Technician** | Voice/text findings | “Bearing is worn and noisy.” |
| **Technician** | Close-out summary | AI creates a clean work summary from findings. |
